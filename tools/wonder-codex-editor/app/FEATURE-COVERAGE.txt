WONDER CODEX EDITOR 1.0.0 - FEATURE GUIDE

SAVES AND NAVIGATION
Automatic account discovery for Steam, GOG and Xbox / Game Pass on Windows;
manual save file/folder opening; save slot and revision selection; reload,
Save As, review of character/account changes, and backup checks.
All original File, Edit, View and Help menu commands remain available.

INVENTORIES AND CHARACTER
Choose the owner and inventory: exosuit, multitool, starship, freighter, vehicle,
storage container, cooking ingredients or Skiff cold storage, as present in the
save. Edit items and slots; add, move, delete, repair, recharge and fill; resize,
enable slots and manage supercharged slots. Global inventory tools remain under
Tools & settings. Exosuit statistics and Units, Nanites and Quicksilver are editable.

FLEET AND EQUIPMENT
Starships and multitools: selection, names, supported types/classes/seeds,
statistics, inventories and import/export.
Corvettes: names and classes, linked-build information, and inventory access
through Ships. Type conversion and deletions that would break linked builds are
blocked. Base JSON includes TAZmd's separately installed Corvette Optimizer.
Freighter: name, type, class, seeds, statistics, inventories, structure backup
and restore. Frigates: names, types, traits, statistics, copy, delete and repair.
Squadron: pilot and ship settings. Vehicles: supported exocraft and their inventories.

BASES, COMPANIONS AND SETTLEMENTS
Bases and storage: base selection, names, structure backup/restore, base-computer
movement and hired NPC settings. Base JSON supports selected-base import, export,
validation, editing and staging. Companions retain name/seed settings and
import/export. Settlements retain statistics, perks and production item editing.

PROGRESSION AND WARP
Unlocks & knowledge: technology, products, words and individual portal glyphs.
Milestones & reputation: journey milestones, counters, race and guild progress.
Account rewards: season, Twitch and platform unlocks, plus account JSON editing.
Missions: progression presets through the Atlantid Drive steps. Supported saves
use Main context, save version 4737-4739 and mission version 40. Presets change
mission state and relevant access/unlock data; they do not install equipment or
grant physical quest items. Other versions or contexts are blocked.
Warp: galaxy selection and portal/galactic/universal address entry, or clicking
glyphs in sequence. Changes target a system, not a precise portal position.
Expedition archive: original launch-date references. Expedition replay is not
available; changing the PC date alone is not a verified replay method. The
editor does not change the system clock or install expedition data.

WONDER CODEX
Local scanning and selected/all-eligible contribution of fauna, flora, minerals,
planets and systems. Owned starships, freighters, frigates and multitools are
listed separately from discoveries with an established origin. Exact-identity
companion matches may enrich fauna records. Corvette assemblies, unmatched
companions, eggs and inventory catalog signals are available for local review
but cannot be submitted by this importer.
Passport approval, site-granted import access and a compatible service are
required for submission. The site may restrict who can approve this editor.
Contributions go to the review queue, not directly to the public catalog.
Only selected records are sent. Ariadne & Capture Companion downloads follow
the site's separate authorization and availability; check the listed build.

JSON AND SETTINGS
Full-save and account JSON access remain alongside the selected-base tools,
including tree/text views, validation, search and import/export. Tools & settings
includes appearance, inventory scaling, global inventory actions, the original
advanced Test Mode command and compatibility reports. Available controls depend
on the selected save and its data.
